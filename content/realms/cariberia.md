## On the Strip of Wonders

The Strip is Cariberia's postcard, and it is gloriously, proudly fake. Walk its length under the neon and you will pass whole replicas of the Eiffel Tower, Big Ben, the Statue of Liberty, the Colosseum, the Hollywood sign, Chichén Itzá, the Burj Khalifa, the Taj Mahal and St Basil's Cathedral, each one built for the joy of the spectacle.

Among them stand four wonders of a different kind: the restored relics. Half of the Sphinx's face, the arm of Christ the Redeemer, a piece of the Great Wall and half of an Easter Island moai are real stone salvaged from the old world, with the missing parts newly built on to make each one whole again. The seam is left visible on purpose, so you can see exactly where the old stone ends and the new build begins.

## Culture

**Fashion, day to night.** Every Cariberian owns two wardrobes. By day it is LA athleisure and sports kit, the unscripted half of the realm. By night it is sequins, flamenco ruffles and Havana silk, the scripted half, cut in 1950s Havana silhouettes and lit up in Day of the Dead marigold. The change happens at sunset, just in time for the Malecón.

**Full volume.** Cariberia is loud where Neuropa is quiet. Neuropa dresses in quiet luxury; Cariberia turns everything up.

## The Day of the Dead

On Reconnection Day, 7 May, Cariberia joins every other realm in the five minutes of noise for the people lost in the Great Reconnecting.

Cariberia also keeps a full day for all its dead, on the traditional Day of the Dead dates, 1 and 2 November. It is the old Día de Muertos, evolved for the new world, honouring the tradition rather than leaving it behind. The marigold arches and ofrendas of the Avenue of Altars are its set, and the celebration is broadcast across the whole realm as a big show, so all of Cariberia remembers its dead together.

## Opening the 2077 season

When the Director drew the realms' order at Thanksmas, Cariberia came out first. The realm of spectacle, film, beauty and sport opens the 2077 Exhibition season, setting a glamorous, showbiz tone from the very first week.

Neuropa, home of storytelling, closes the season in week ten, right before the final. So this year's Exhibition is bookended beautifully: the screen opens it, and the page closes it.
