  - id: reconnection
    eyebrow: 7 May
    heading: Reconnection Day
    text: |
      On 7 May we remember the Great Reconnecting, the day our world became one. It is both a memorial and a celebration, and it opens the Exhibition season every year.

      We believe grief is best shared, and that feelings should be felt, never suppressed. So instead of a minute’s silence, the whole world makes five minutes of noise. We play music, shout into the void, sing the names of the people we lost and speak loudly to the universe, in case they can hear us. Then the parade begins.
    items:

    text: |
      Cariberia joins every other realm in the five minutes of noise on Reconnection Day. It also keeps a day of its own: a full day for all of its dead, on the traditional Day of the Dead dates of 1 and 2 November.
