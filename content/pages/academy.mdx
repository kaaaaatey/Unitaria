## The seat of the world

The Academy is the world's great town hall, where the Director and the ten realm representatives sit at the round table. It is also the world's favourite broadcast, simply called *The Academy*. The name is a promise: all of us, forever growing, learning and evolving.

It stands in white marble and gold on clifftops above the sea, crowned by a vast glass-and-gold dome. Slender towers, sweeping balconies, cypress and topiary, waterfalls and long reflecting pools lead up grand stairways. Inside, the Great Hall rises in soaring arches hung with the four house banners around a towering column of light. The round table sits beneath a star-map dome, the Grand Library glows with golden globes, and arched windows open onto the sea.

## The compass

<div class="not-prose my-10 flex justify-center text-gold-deep"><CompassStar size={120} label="The Academy's gold eight-pointed compass star" /></div>

The Academy's symbol is a gold, eight-pointed compass star, for guidance. You'll find it on banners, on robes and engraved on bands: the Academy's promise to point every one of us towards our truest direction.

## Growing up

**The School of Life, before sixteen.** Childhood is the classroom. Points tasks teach every young person to grow into a capable adult, and there is no single test: a whole childhood of helping, learning and doing shows what each child is naturally good at.

**The Academy year, at sixteen.** Every sixteen-year-old spends one guaranteed year at an Academy, a little like sixth form. Academies stand in every realm, and every one mixes students from all ten, so every teenager spends a year away from home with people from everywhere. The whole year is broadcast, so communities can watch their own children grow, and cheer them on with points.

No child knows their leaderboard score until they turn sixteen. Like waiting for a letter, you never know whether this might be your year.

## The Golden Prospects

At fifteen, every child submits an entry to the wider network, to be judged and championed with points by their community. The results stay hidden, so no child loses hope and no child gains an edge.

From every realm, **four** are chosen for the **Grand Academy** in Neuropa: **forty** in all, chosen purely for their effort and their contribution to their community. They are the **Golden Prospects**, and the whole world watches their year.

## The Compass Ceremony

<div class="not-prose my-10 flex justify-center"><Compass size="min(80vw, 360px)" class="text-gold-deep" /></div>

The Academy year opens with the Compass Ceremony. One by one, each student touches the great golden compass. It spins, and points to one of four houses, each holding one of the Academy's four rules for the world: **Polaris** for Knowledge, **Aurora** for Wonder, **Lyra** for Harmony and **Orion** for Courage.

At the Grand Academy, the forty are sorted into four houses of ten, with one student from every realm in each. They rise or fall together. Your realm is where you're from. Your house is who you are.

## The Exhibition season

The Academy year ends with the Exhibition: a World's Fair in marble pavilions, broadcast across all three Continuents, with music, pomp and a speech from the Director. Every sixteen-year-old submits something for growth and innovation in their passion, entered in their realm's great callings.
