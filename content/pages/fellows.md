    eyebrow: The Director
    heading: Benedict Reverington
    text: |
      Not a ruler. A guide.

      Long before the Great Reconnecting, Benedict Reverington was the voice of ordinary people: followed, trusted and loved for his innovation and his generosity, and never shy about saying the old system was failing. He was twenty-one when the world broke apart. He lost his father and his older brother that day, as so many of us lost the people we loved.

      When the dust settled, the world asked him to lead "while it found its feet". Fifty years on, he still offers to hand over the reins, and the world still won't hear of it.

      He sits at the round table as one voice among ten realm representatives. He does a task every day, like everyone else, always in the learning section with the children. He loved the World's Fairs of old, so he gave us the Exhibition. And he is an Aurora, the house's founding Fellow, of course.

      His appearances are rare, which is why every one feels like an event. Look for him at the Gratitude Parade at Thanksmas, and on stage at the Grand Final.
  - id: seven
    eyebrow: The Academy's children
    heading: The Seven
    text: |
      Seven children were born on 7 May 2027, the day the world was remade, and lived through everything that followed. The Academy took them in and raised them as its own, while the Director was still in his twenties: part wards, part family, part inner circle.

      They are our living reminder of the birth, the loss and the renewal of the new world. On 7 May 2077 they turn fifty, at the heart of the Golden Jubilee, and the Square of the Seven in Neuropa will be the loudest place on Earth.
