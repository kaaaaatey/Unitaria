    heading: Benedict Reverington
    text: |
      Not a ruler. A guide.

      Long before the Great Reconnecting, Benedict Reverington was a socialite turned influencer, and then something rarer: the head of popular culture who became the voice of the people. He was followed, trusted and loved for his innovation and his generosity. He was anti-billionaire and anti-government, and never shy about saying the old system was failing.

      Ask anyone who has met him and they will tell you about the charm first, and then the cheek. He is famously, sometimes a little too, honest. And he has a gift that no title could give him: he can sit with anyone, from a realm representative to a seven-year-old on a gold learning task, and truly understand them.

      He sits at the round table as one voice among ten realm representatives. He does a task every day, like everyone else, always in the learning section with the children. He loved the World's Fairs of old, so he gave us the Exhibition. And he is an Aurora, the house's founding Fellow, of course.

    heading: Always on the screens. Never on every channel.
    text: |
      You will see the Director every day, on the great screens of the Broadcast Quarter and the screens of every realm. But you will never find him on every channel at once. He keeps his appearances rare, which is why every one feels like an event.

      Think of a royal balcony wave, or an unannounced walkabout through a crowd that suddenly realises who is shaking their hand. When he appears, the whole world says the same thing: "Look, he's here! Aren't we lucky he's here."

      Look for him at the Gratitude Parade at Thanksmas, and on stage at the Grand Final.

    eyebrow: The Academy's children
    heading: The Seven
    text: |
      On 7 May 2027, the day the world was remade, babies were born into a world turned upside down. Of the babies born that day, seven survived. The Academy took them in and raised them as its own: part wards, part siblings, part inner circle. We call them the Seven, for the seventh of May.

      They were raised while the Director was still in his twenties, and he was their first teacher. The Academy's name has never been just a name.

      They are our living reminder of the birth, the loss and the renewal of the new world. On 7 May 2077 they turn fifty, at the heart of the Golden Jubilee, and the Square of the Seven in Neuropa will be the loudest place on Earth.
