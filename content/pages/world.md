## The Great Reconnecting

On **7 May 2027**, an undersea catastrophe beyond any human control nearly ended everything. We survived it, and many of us believe we were saved by something greater than ourselves.

It came in three movements:

- **The Shake.** Hours of quakes and tsunamis.
- **The Drift.** Weeks and months of continents grinding together.
- **The Settling.** Years of aftershocks, new land rising and coastlines finally holding.

We lost so much. The world was home to more than eight billion people; today it is home to about one and a half billion. The loss touched every Continuent, so no culture was erased.

## What we built

Most of the old governments were gone. Survivors came together in emergency coalitions, one for each Continuent. Armies became the **Restoration Corp**, turning military know-how into relief. Nuclear weapons were dismantled in public ceremonies, swords into ploughshares, while the whole world watched.

Then we reorganised. Instead of borders drawn by rulers, our realms grew around the unique skills and offerings of their land and the people who survived there. Cultures, innovations, lives and livelihoods blended, and every system the old world relied on was made new. Most of the old world's wealth was poured into the rebuild, so old money largely melted away into the new world we were building together.

## Things have to change

The aftermath was awful, and it was also full of humanity: community, purpose, belonging and duty. People had felt that spirit before, after wars and after the pandemic, and every time it faded once life went "back to normal". Jobs to do, rent to pay, the clean-up finished, and everyone drifted apart again.

This time, nobody went back. This was a clean slate, not a little rearranging of the old ways. A whole generation, tired of a system that never kept its promises, agreed on one thing: **things have to change**. The Director became the voice of that message, and the world we live in grew from it.

The lesson we carry is simple. Humanity really does thrive together.

## The money years

In about **2032**, the Director abolished money, calling it "the root of all evil". For a while, it worked. By about **2034** the moneyless experiment had collapsed, because we learned that even a kind world needs an economy to keep things in order.

So from about **2035** we agreed on something better suited to us: **points**. Points reward people for rebuilding the world together, not for personal greed. They arrived with the LiFE band and the School of Life, and they are how our world has worked ever since.

## Living memory

The Great Reconnecting is still within living memory. Our grandparents remember the world before, when oceans lay between the continents. Our parents remember the aftermath and the rebuild. Today's teenagers were born after, into a world that has only ever been one. Ask an elder about the old world and you will hear about places that are no longer where they used to be, and some that are no longer anywhere at all. We treasure those stories.

## Faith, and kindness between faiths

Religion grew after the Reconnecting, and spirituality grew even more. After so much loss, people needed something to pray to, and many simply could not believe they had survived. Faiths flourished in that wonder.

What grew alongside them was grace. People were more grateful to hold on to their own faith than to tear down one another's, and an openness between faiths took root. It is one of the things we are proudest of.

## One voice, many languages

Everyone kept their own language, and our bands translate for us in real time. Young people have gone a step further and speak a blended slang, mixing words from every culture into something all their own. Neuropa is the exception: there, language is meant to be inclusive, and slang isn't really a thing.

We think of people as blends, too. Nobody is only one thing, and every community holds a little of everything. Rehoming is always a choice: you can move to the community that suits your soul.

And on the new land, purple forests and glowing landscapes appeared that no one fully understands yet. Familiar places turn up somewhere new, a national park with purple trees, a city whose transport runs through the sky.

## One world, within reach

Today the oceans that once divided us are gone. One land holds **three Continuents** and **ten realms**, linked by solar monorails, so every culture is on every doorstep. We call them Continuents to honour the continents that merged to make them: each one is a continuation of the continents that came before, new but never forgotten. Each realm is named for the cultures that came together within it, and each has its own North: the ethos it lives by.
