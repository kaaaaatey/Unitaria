On 7 May 2027, an undersea catastrophe beyond any human control shook the Earth. Over hours came the Shake. Over months came the Drift. Over years came the Settling.

When the waters stilled, the oceans that once divided us were gone. In their place: one land, three Continuents and ten realms, with every culture on every doorstep. We call it **the Great Reconnecting**, and we give thanks for it every day.
