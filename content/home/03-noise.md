heading: Five minutes of noise
---
The old world kept silence for its dead. We make noise.

Every Reconnection Day, for five minutes, everyone, everywhere, lets it out: playing music, shouting into the void, singing the names of the ones we lost, speaking loudly to the universe. Grief is best shared, and feelings are for feeling.

Nowhere is louder than the Square of the Seven in Neuropa. Then the noise falls away, and the season begins.
