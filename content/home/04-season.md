Every spring, the whole world turns to the Academy. Eleven weeks of pomp, music and marvels, one realm at a time, broadcast across all three Continuents.

The order is never planned. Each Thanksmas, during the Gratitude Parade, the Director draws every realm's week truly at random, live in front of the whole world, and so the year's two great days are linked: Thanksmas sets the order, and Reconnection Day starts the season. For 2077, Cariberia's spectacle opens and Neuropa's storytelling closes, the screen first and the page last.
