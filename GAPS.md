## 2. Brand and IP risks

- **"Fire Fox".** Aurora's creature is "the Fire Fox", which is very close to a real software brand. The brief bans real brands. The Finnish *revontulet* ("fox fires") root is lovely; the site could say "the fox of the northern lights" until the name is settled. 🔴 Sprint 2
- **Real people on the Walk of Innovation.** The plaque lists are marked "possible", not decided, and putting real people (some living) on a public page is a likeness question. Until decided, the Neuropa page mentions the Walk without naming plaques. 🟠 Sprint 2
- **Real-world anchors** (Duolingo, the Eras Tour, Humane AI Pin and so on) are writers' notes and never appear in in-world copy.
